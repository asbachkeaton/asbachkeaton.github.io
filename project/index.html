Senior Project Title

This project investigated whether Topological Data Analysis (TDA) could identify structural similarities between professional Counter-Strike teams and help predict the best team of 2024. Using persistence barcodes and mathematical distance calculations on player statistics, I found that TDA revealed useful similarities but was most effective when combined with traditional measures such as tournament performance, rankings, and prize money.

Overview

  • Context: My interest in mathematics, data analysis, and competitive Counter-Strike motivated me to explore whether TDA could uncover useful patterns in team performance.
  • Question: The project investigated whether Topological Data Analysis could identify structural similarities between professional Counter-Strike teams and help predict which team would be the strongest in 2024.
  • Result: The key insight was that TDA is most useful as a tool for translating complex data into meaningful patterns, rather than as a standalone prediction method.

Methods

I applied persistent homology and persistence barcodes to player statistics, using mathematical distance calculations to compare the underlying structure of professional Counter-Strike teams. I used Ripser to generate the persistence barcodes and checked the results by comparing multiple teams against the 2023 Team of the Year, then cross-referencing the findings with tournament results, rankings, and prize money.

Results

  • Result 1: TDA identified measurable structural similarities between top Counter-Strike teams, with distance calculations showing how closely each team’s data resembled the 2023 Team of the Year.
  • Result 2: The analysis showed that TDA can uncover useful patterns, but combining it with tournament results, rankings, and prize money provided a stronger basis for evaluating team performance.
  • Limitations / assumptions: The analysis used a relatively small dataset and did not initially account for factors such as tournament placements, prize money, or time spent ranked in the Top 10, limiting TDA’s ability to make a standalone prediction.
